Privacy

The short version: what you log in TrinityMate stays on your phone, and this website doesn't track you.

The TrinityMate app

Everything you enter in TrinityMate, such as symptoms, triggers, foods, medicines, heart rate readings, joint events and notes, is saved only on your own device. There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We can't see your data and we don't receive it.

When you save a backup, export a file or print a summary, you choose where it goes. To delete everything, use Erase all data in the app's settings.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Buying the app happens on Etsy, under Etsy's own privacy policy.

TrinityMate is a tracker app for MCAS, EDS and POTS, made by HealthAppStore in Exeter, England.

TrinityMate and this site are for tracking, organising and general information. They aren't medical advice, can't diagnose anything and don't replace your doctor. If you're worried about symptoms, speak to a healthcare professional. In an emergency, call 999.
